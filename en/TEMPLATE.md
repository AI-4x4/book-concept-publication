# A Small Book: Fillable Outline

Albert Toma · Supplement to the full edition

Fill only what the current task needs. An unanswered question remains open; a sample answer does not become a fact.

## Intent

What should be achieved, for whom, and why? Which essential properties define success? What lies outside this task?

## Surrounding Context

Who states the intent, and who receives the result? Which meanings, goals, preferences, and conditions matter to this task? What was stated directly and what was inferred from materials; where is the source, and what remains unverified?

If the task concerns a company, where is its Book: separate or combined with the User's Book? What are the company's goals, commitments, and current authority? Which propositions are personal, which are corporate, and who is permitted to see this information?

## Concepts and Grounds

What do the key terms mean? What information has been checked, and under what conditions? What is an assumption? What remains unknown?

## Root Grounds and Derived Materials

Which Book is the root reference for this system and scope? Which version applies? Which specifications, architectural descriptions, plans, and checks are derived from it? Where are their grounds, assumptions, and adoption statuses? Which original evidence is retained separately?

Who may change the intent and grounds? How can a discrepancy between a derived document and the Book, or an error in the Book itself, be detected? What would an adopted change affect, how would compatibility be checked, and which consequences cannot be undone by reverting documents?

## Decisions and Relationships

What has been adopted, and who may change it? Why was this method selected? Which grounds support the decision, and which requirements does it realize? What alternatives were considered?

## Work and Evaluation

What is the next complete result? Who receives it, and who acts? Which ordinary case, failure, or plausible substitution would distinguish successful execution from a superficially similar outcome?

## Observed Result

What has actually been obtained? Which properties have been checked, and which remain unknown? Can the recipient use the result under the required conditions?

## Changes and Continuation

What changed: intent, a factual premise, or the implementation method? Which dependent decisions need reconsideration? What is preserved, and which next step is actually supported?
