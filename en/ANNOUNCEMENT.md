# The Book: preserving intent as a project changes

An idea moves through discussions, requirements, decisions, and implementation. At each transition, its meaning can change unnoticed, even when individual tasks are completed correctly.

In this basic Book concept, I describe an approach to maintaining the connection between intent, the grounds for decisions, and verified results. The Book develops with the project; the idea and its technical implementation remain distinct and connected.

The edition includes an article and a hypothetical worked example. It discusses contradictions, completeness, and the limits of verification. This is a conceptual account: its effectiveness remains to be demonstrated on specific tasks.
